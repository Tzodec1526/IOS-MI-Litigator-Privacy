MI Litigator — Privacy Policy

Updated September 28, 2026

This policy covers MI Litigator version 1.1.3 and the native version 2.0.1, including TestFlight builds. It applies to both versions while the update is being reviewed and after release. Features that differ by version are identified below.

Information handled by the app

MI Litigator is a reference app for Michigan and federal court rules. Both versions have no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking. The app does not send your searches, saved rules, recently viewed rules, or appearance preferences to the developer or an app-operated server. The developer does not sell this information.

The app stores saved rules, a list of recently viewed rules, and appearance preferences locally on your device. Search questions are processed on your device and are not saved as a search-history list. Optional email feedback is described separately below.

Search and Apple Intelligence

In both versions, rule content and the MiniLM search model are bundled with the app. Reading, keyword search, and bundled-model search work offline.

Both versions can also use Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework on supported devices. Apple manages that system model and its download through Apple Intelligence. Availability depends on your device, operating system, and Apple Intelligence settings. The app does not send your search questions to a remote AI service.

Local data, backups, and deletion

You can empty the current saved-rule and recent-rule lists in Settings:

  • Version 1.1.3: Clear saved & recents.
  • Version 2.0.1: Clear saved rules and recent history.

Clearing those lists does not reset your appearance preference. When you update from version 1.1.3, version 2.0.1 imports existing saved rules, recents, and appearance preferences on your device. The original local data from the older version may remain after the import.

Local preferences and any remaining app data are retained until you remove them or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading the app preserves its documents and data. iOS may include app data in iCloud or computer backups, depending on your settings. Removing local data does not necessarily remove earlier backups. Manage or delete those backups using Apple or computer backup controls. The developer cannot access your local app data or device backups.

Sharing, clipboard, and external links

Both versions let you share a rule summary and copy a citation. Share opens iOS sharing controls, and you choose the destination. Copy cite places the citation on the system clipboard. Version 2.0.1 also lets you share a deadline calculation and open official-source websites.

Both versions can open this privacy page in your browser. External websites and apps you choose to use handle information under their own privacy policies. These actions occur only when you choose them.

Optional email feedback

Send feedback opens your mail app with an editable draft that includes the app version. Nothing is emailed until you choose to send it. If you email the developer, the developer receives your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to include. Your email provider also handles that message under its own policy.

Feedback is used to respond to your request and address the issue you report. Support correspondence is retained as needed for those purposes or as required by law. You can request deletion by emailing the contact below. Sending feedback is optional and is not required to use the app.
